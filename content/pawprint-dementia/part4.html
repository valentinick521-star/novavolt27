Why PawPrint Uses a 90-Day Window

You should be skeptical of any product telling you that a complicated age-related problem is going to disappear overnight.

A better approach is consistency plus observation.

If you decide to try a new nutritional strategy, keep everything else as stable as reasonably possible and pay attention to the behaviors you actually care about.

For example:

Weeks 1–4

Establish the routine.

Will your dog take it consistently?

Are you actually able to give the intended serving every day without turning breakfast into a negotiation?

Weeks 5–8

Watch the ordinary behaviors.

Sleep.

Interaction.

Interest in normal routines.

How often the dog seems disoriented.

Write it down instead of trusting memory.

Weeks 9–12

Look back at the record.

Is there enough meaningful difference that you want to continue?

One Important Step Before You Try Any Supplement

New confusion or behavioral change deserves veterinary attention.

Cognitive dysfunction can overlap with other problems, including sensory loss, pain and neurological or metabolic conditions.

A supplement should not become an excuse to skip that evaluation.

But once serious alternatives have been considered, there is nothing unreasonable about becoming more deliberate about the nutritional strategy you choose.

And that's ultimately the point.
